Researcher & Production

The GDP Podcast

A government-focused podcast. Prepared research briefs and interview documents for the host ahead of each episode.

Know More

On the GDP Podcast, I was the only researcher, building every guest's research from scratch. For each one, I'd start with the basics: who they are, their family, their education, the arc of their career, pulled together from old interviews, articles, YouTube videos, their social media, LinkedIn, and government sources where relevant.

Then the research turned to policy. The show's whole idea was to carry government schemes and policy to a wider audience through people who actually lived them: politicians, actors, CEOs, astronauts, authors, military officers, bureaucrats, whoever the guest happened to be. I'd dig into which schemes they'd used or worked on, something like Startup India or semiconductor policy, and get the real numbers behind it, not just the headline version.

I also drafted talking points and a rough shape for each interview, which the senior team would review and adjust. Beyond research, I helped cut long interviews down into reels for social media, working with the editor to find the moments that would pull people toward the full episode, and did a pass for grammar and factual slips before anything went out.

Director's Assistant, Post-Production Supervisor & PR Content

The Great Shamsuddin Family

Jio Hotstar. Coordinated across departments from pre-production through final delivery, and worked with the Jio Hotstar PR team writing promotional and PR scripts for the release campaign.

Know More

My role kept growing as the production did. In pre-production in Delhi, I worked as director's assistant: researching character and period detail, and making sure costume, makeup, camera, direction, production, and sound all stayed aligned with the director's vision. On shoot days, I was there to support the director through last-minute script changes and keep the day moving.

When the production shifted to Mumbai for post, I stayed on as director's assistant, tracking edit notes and coordinating between edit, sound, and DI, then taking on censor board submissions. That grew into a promotion to post-production supervisor, where I made sure the censor certificate came through on time, ran point on screenings across Delhi, Mumbai, and eventually worldwide, and became the main contact between our team and Jio Hotstar for every deliverable.

Later, I also ran the PR and social media side: daily calls with the Jio Hotstar team, coordinating between the actors and the network, and making sure every script, video, and collaboration went out on schedule. My work on the film officially wrapped in January 2026, and it's still screening internationally.
